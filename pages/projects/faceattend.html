FaceAttend.

A webcam-based face recognition attendance tool that records recognized identities with timestamps.

PythonOpenCVNumPyFace recognition

The project

From face detection to an attendance record.

FaceAttend captures images from a webcam, compares detected faces against prepared encodings, and logs recognized attendance with timestamps in CSV files.

The project separates image capture, face encoding, and recognition into individual steps to keep the workflow understandable and maintainable.

What I built

  • Built webcam-based face detection and recognition with OpenCV and the face recognition library.
  • Created a workflow for collecting and encoding registered faces.
  • Recorded recognized attendance in timestamped CSV files.
  • Organized the code into capture, encoding, and recognition components.
