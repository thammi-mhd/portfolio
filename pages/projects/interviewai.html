The project

Practice interviews with actionable feedback.

InterviewAI lets candidates practice role-specific technical and HR interviews. It generates questions for the selected role and evaluates written or spoken answers with AI-powered feedback.

The modular FastAPI backend connects to PostgreSQL through SQLAlchemy and supports interview sessions, authentication, and response processing. Voice submissions are transcribed for evaluation, with session history and feedback available to the candidate.

What I built

  • Designed backend API modules for authentication, interview sessions, and AI response evaluation.
  • Integrated Gemini for role-specific question generation and candidate feedback.
  • Added voice-response processing using SpeechRecognition and Pydub.
  • Applied JWT authentication, OTP verification, password hashing, Pydantic validation, and rate limiting.
